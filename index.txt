<h2>LED mirrors vs ordinary mirrors</h2>
<p>An ordinary mirror only reflects the light that&#39;s already in the room, so you see yourself in whatever shadows the ceiling light creates. An LED mirror adds light at face height, from the front, which is why it&#39;s so much better for shaving, skincare, makeup and getting dressed. It also replaces a separate wall light above the basin or dressing table, which means one less fitting to buy and one less switch on the wall.</p>
<p>LED mirrors are also efficient. A typical GlowGlass mirror uses about as much electricity as one energy-saver bulb, and it runs happily on a UPS or inverter during load-shedding.</p>
