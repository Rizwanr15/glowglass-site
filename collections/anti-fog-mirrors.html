How does an anti-fog mirror work?

A thin heating pad is fixed to the back of the glass. When the mirror light is switched on, the pad warms the glass a few degrees above the temperature of the steam, so condensation cannot form. After a hot shower the mirror stays clear instead of fogging over.

Is an anti-fog mirror worth it?

In small bathrooms, bathrooms without a window and homes where geysers run hot in winter, anti-fog saves wiping the glass every morning and stops water streaks. The pad uses little electricity and only runs while the mirror is on.

Cleaning an anti-fog mirror

Clean it like any mirror: switch off, spray a mild glass cleaner onto a soft cloth (not directly onto the glass) and wipe. Avoid abrasive pads. There is nothing to maintain on the pad itself.

Quick buying tips

  • Anti-fog matters most in small bathrooms with poor ventilation.
  • The pad uses little power and switches on with the light.

Anti-Fog Bathroom Mirrors: questions

Quick answers. Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp.

How does an anti-fog mirror work?

A heating pad behind the glass keeps it slightly warmer than the steam, so condensation does not form.

Does anti-fog use a lot of electricity?

No, it uses roughly as much as a small bulb, and only while the mirror is on.

Can anti-fog be added later?

It is fitted during production, so choose it when you order.

Is anti-fog safe in a bathroom?

Yes. The pad is low-power and sealed behind the glass. Keep the mirror out of direct shower spray.
