Wall mirror designs for every room

Bedroom: a tall LED or framed mirror for dressing, or a round mirror above the dressing table. Backlit mirrors give a soft glow that works as a night light.

Living room: a large round, arch or irregular mirror above the sofa or console becomes the focal point and bounces light around the room. Decorative sets of small round or rhombus mirrors make a feature wall without a single large piece.

Entrance and hallway: a slim arch or tall capsule widens a narrow corridor and gives guests a last look before they leave.

Bathroom: an LED mirror with anti-fog and a touch dimmer is the practical choice. See our bathroom mirror designs for more.

Modern, wooden-look and decorative styles

Modern wall mirrors are frameless or have slim metal frames in black, gold, rose gold or silver. For a warmer, wooden-look interior, pair a gold or rose-gold framed mirror with wood furniture, or choose an organic irregular shape that softens the room.

Decorative wall mirrors are about pattern and arrangement: etched LED designs, sunburst rings and mirror sets you can arrange in a grid or cluster.

Wall mirror prices in Pakistan

Decorative mirror sets start from Rs 2,199. Small framed mirrors start around Rs 3,200, LED wall mirrors from Rs 4,500, and large framed LED mirrors run to about Rs 41,000. The full list is on our mirror price page.

Hanging height and size

Hang the centre of a wall mirror at roughly eye level, 60 to 65 inches from the floor. Above furniture, leave 6 to 10 inches between the top of the furniture and the bottom of the mirror. On an empty wall, a mirror between half and two-thirds of the wall width looks balanced.

Quick buying tips

  • In a bedroom, hang the mirror opposite or beside a window to double the daylight.
  • Above a sofa or console, choose a mirror about two-thirds the width of the furniture.
  • For a lounge feature wall, group small round or rhombus mirrors rather than one large one.

A tall LED or framed mirror for dressing, or a round mirror above a dressing table. LED backlit designs double as a soft night light.
