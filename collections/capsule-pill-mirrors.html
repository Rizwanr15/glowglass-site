Why capsule mirrors are so popular

The capsule has become the signature shape of modern bathrooms. Its straight sides line up with tiles and vanities, while the rounded ends soften the room. It photographs well too, which is why you see it so often in renovated homes and cafés.

Wall capsules and full-length capsules

For a bathroom, a 20 × 32 or 24 × 36 inch capsule above a single basin is the usual choice. For a bedroom or hallway, a tall capsule from 18 × 60 to 24 × 72 inches works as a full-length dressing mirror, wall-mounted or leaned.

Both come with an LED backlight and touch dimmer, and bathroom models can have anti-fog.

Capsule vs oval vs rectangle

A capsule is wider at the ends than an oval, so it gives more usable mirror for the same width. Compared with a rectangle it has no corners, which makes a small bathroom feel softer and less crowded.

Sizes, placement and lighting

Choose a capsule a few inches narrower than the basin or vanity below it, and hang it with the centre at eye level. In a hallway, place a full-length capsule where it reflects a window or light source to brighten the space. For a pair of basins, two matching capsules look better than one wide one.

Warm 3000K light gives a cosy, hotel-like glow; neutral 4000K is the most accurate for makeup; cool 6000K is brightest. Our 3-in-1 option lets you switch between all three with a tap.

Quick buying tips

  • Use a capsule over a narrow vanity or in a hallway.
  • Tall capsules double as a soft ambient light when backlit.
