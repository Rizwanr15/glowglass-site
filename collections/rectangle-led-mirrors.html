Rectangle LED mirror sizes explained

Small rectangles (18 × 24 to 24 × 30 inches) suit powder rooms, guest bathrooms and servant quarters, where the basin is narrow and the wall is short. Medium sizes (24 × 36 to 30 × 48 inches) are the standard choice above a single vanity. Large rectangles (36 × 48 to 36 × 60 inches and wider) cover a double vanity or a dressing wall.

If your wall falls between sizes, we cut to the inch. Measure the space twice, note the position of the power point, and send both on WhatsApp.

Backlit, front-lit or both

A backlit rectangle sits a few centimetres off the wall and throws a halo around its edge. It is the softer, more atmospheric option and doubles as a night light.

A front-lit rectangle has a frosted band on the face of the glass. The light lands on your face, so it is better for shaving, makeup and contact lenses. Our Duo design does both, and the Frame-Line and pattern designs light an etched line or motif.

Black, framed and frameless styles

Frameless rectangles with polished edges are the cleanest look and the easiest to keep clean. A thin black painted border gives a framed look without extra depth, and suits black taps and shower screens. Slim metal frames in black, gold, rose gold or silver suit hotel-style and classic bathrooms.

Hanging height and placement

Hang the mirror so its centre is roughly at eye level, usually 60 to 65 inches from the floor, with the bottom edge 5 to 10 inches above the basin or tap. Leave a few inches either side so it does not crowd wall lights or cabinets.

Quick buying tips

  • Choose a width 2 to 4 inches narrower than the vanity below it.
  • Hang portrait over a single basin and landscape over a double vanity.
  • Front-lit borders are best for shaving and makeup; backlit designs give a softer, ambient glow.

Rectangle LED Mirrors: questions

Quick answers. Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp.

What size rectangle LED mirror do I need for my bathroom?

Measure the vanity width and pick a mirror slightly narrower. For a 24 inch basin, 20 × 28 or 24 × 36 inches works; for a 48 inch double vanity, 36 × 48 or wider in landscape.

Can a rectangle LED mirror hang horizontally?

Yes. Tell us when you order so the touch sensor and wiring are placed for landscape hanging.

Which is better, backlit or front-lit?

Front-lit puts more light on your face, which helps for makeup and shaving. Backlit gives a halo on the wall and softer room light. Some models do both.

What is the cheapest rectangle LED mirror?

Our Frame-Line and Compact rectangles start from Rs 4,500 to Rs 6,400 in small sizes.

How much does a rectangle LED mirror cost in Pakistan?

Our rectangle LED mirrors start from Rs 4,500 for 18 × 24 inches. Popular bathroom sizes such as 24 × 36 inches are Rs 9,000 to Rs 15,000, depending on the design and lighting.

Can a rectangle LED mirror have Bluetooth?

Yes, many of our rectangles can take a built-in Bluetooth speaker as an option.
