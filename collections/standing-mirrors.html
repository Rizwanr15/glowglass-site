Standing, floor and long mirrors

People search for the same thing under different names: standing mirror, floor mirror, long mirror and full-length mirror. All of them are tall mirrors that show you head to toe. A standing mirror has its own stand or frame so it can stand on the floor; a full-length wall mirror is fixed to the wall at the same height.

Our designs work both ways: lean them against the wall with the stand, or fix them to the wall with the fixings supplied.

Standing mirrors with LED lights

An LED standing mirror lights your outfit evenly, which helps at night or in a dark dressing room. The backlight also acts as ambient lighting for a bedroom corner. All LED designs have a touch dimmer and three light colours.

Choosing the size

Height matters more than width. 60 inches is the minimum for a head-to-toe view for most adults; 66 to 72 inches is better for tall people or if you want to see shoes from a distance. 18 inches wide fits behind doors and in narrow corners; 24 to 36 inches wide suits a dressing room.

Standing mirror price in Pakistan

Tall LED mirrors start from Rs 11,250. Aluminium-frame standing mirrors without lights start at Rs 15,999. Framed LED and statement designs such as the Pond mirror run from about Rs 28,000 to Rs 41,600. Delivery and fitting are free in Lahore.

Quick buying tips

  • Look for at least 60 inches of height to see yourself head to toe.
  • Corner placement saves floor space and gives a wider reflection of the room.
  • Lean-style mirrors can also be fixed to the wall later if you prefer.

What is the price of a standing mirror in Pakistan?

Our full-length and standing mirrors start from Rs 11,250 for a tall LED mirror. Aluminium-frame standing mirrors start at Rs 15,999, and framed LED standing mirrors run to about Rs 41,600.

Is a standing mirror stable?

Yes. Our standing mirrors have a back stand or a weighted frame, and we can add a wall anchor for homes with small children.

What size standing mirror should I buy?

For most adults, 18 × 60 or 24 × 60 inches is enough. Choose 24 × 72 inches or larger if you want to see your full outfit with shoes from a distance.

Do standing mirrors come with lights?

Yes, our LED standing mirrors have a touch dimmer and three light colours.

What is the difference between a standing mirror and a full-length mirror?

A standing mirror has its own stand or frame and stands on the floor. A full-length mirror is any mirror tall enough to show you head to toe, standing or wall-mounted.
