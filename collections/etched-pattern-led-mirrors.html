Designer patterns in light

Etched LED mirrors have a pattern sandblasted into the silver backing. When the LEDs behind it switch on, light passes only through the etched areas, so the pattern glows. Lattice and swirl rings frame round mirrors; vines, ornaments and pixels decorate rectangles.

Choosing a pattern for your room

Geometric patterns such as lattice, pixel and frame-line suit modern bathrooms and dark tiles. Floral vines and ornamental corners suit classic or traditional interiors. Sunburst and swirl rings work in salons and dressing rooms, where the pattern becomes a backdrop for photos.

Sizes and prices

Etched round mirrors come in 24, 30 and 36 inch diameters, from Rs 6,000 to Rs 13,500. Patterned rectangles come in 18 × 30 to 30 × 54 inches, from around Rs 12,800 to Rs 16,000. Bluetooth is optional on the rectangle designs.

Every etched mirror is made to order in Lahore in about 7 days and fitted free in Lahore. For other cities, it ships by insured courier with fixings and a fitting guide.

Care and lifespan

Because the etching is on the back of the glass, the front stays smooth and cleans like any mirror. The LED strips are rated for tens of thousands of hours, and the strip, driver and sensor are covered by our 1-year warranty.

Quick buying tips

  • Patterned mirrors look best on a plain or dark tiled wall.
  • Warm light brings out ornamental patterns; cool light suits geometric ones.
