What affects the price of a mirror

Size is the biggest factor: price rises with the area of glass. Shape is next, because curves and irregular outlines take longer to cut and polish than straight edges. Lighting adds the LED strip, driver and touch sensor; anti-fog adds a heating pad; Bluetooth adds a speaker. Frames add material and finishing.

Glass quality matters too. We use 5 mm silver-backed glass with polished edges, which is thicker and clearer than the thin glass in many low-cost mirrors.

Price ranges by type

Decorative mirror sets: Rs 2,199 to Rs 8,000. Framed wall mirrors without lights: from about Rs 3,200. LED bathroom and wall mirrors: Rs 4,500 to about Rs 26,500 for frameless designs. Framed LED mirrors: about Rs 20,500 to Rs 35,500. Full-length and standing mirrors: Rs 11,250 to about Rs 41,600. Hollywood vanity mirrors: from Rs 27,500.

Getting an exact price

For a standard size, the price on each product page is the price you pay. For a custom size, send the width, height, shape and options on WhatsApp and we reply with an exact price and delivery date, usually within an hour during working hours.

Quick buying tips

  • Price rises with area: a 36 × 48 inch mirror has twice the glass of a 24 × 36 inch one.
  • LED lighting, anti-fog and Bluetooth add to the price of a plain mirror.
  • Framed mirrors cost more than frameless ones of the same size.

Mirror Price in Pakistan: questions

Quick answers. Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp.

What is the price of a mirror in Pakistan?

Decorative mirror sets start from Rs 2,199, small framed mirrors from about Rs 3,200, LED mirrors from Rs 4,500 and full-length LED mirrors from Rs 11,250. Large framed LED mirrors run to about Rs 41,000.

What is the mirror price per square foot in Pakistan?

For a frameless LED mirror it works out at very roughly Rs 1,500 to Rs 2,500 per square foot, depending on shape and options. Plain glass without lights costs much less. Ask for a quote for your exact size.

What is the price of a bathroom mirror in Pakistan?

Our LED bathroom mirrors start from Rs 4,500 in small sizes; standard 24 × 36 inch bathroom mirrors are about Rs 9,000 to Rs 15,000.

Why are LED mirrors more expensive than plain mirrors?

They include an LED strip, a driver, a touch sensor and often an anti-fog pad, plus the wiring and testing that go with them.

Do prices include delivery?

Delivery and fitting are free in Lahore. For other cities, courier charges depend on size and are confirmed in your quote.
