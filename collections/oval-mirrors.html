Gold, black and frameless oval mirrors

Gold oval mirrors are the most requested finish in Pakistani homes, especially with brass or gold taps. Black frames suit modern bathrooms with matt black fittings. Frameless backlit ovals let the glow do the work and suit any colour scheme.

Large, small and long ovals

A small oval (around 18 × 30 inches) works above a powder-room basin or beside a door. Standard ovals (24 × 36 inches) suit most single vanities. Long ovals and slim ovals add height to a low ceiling; large ovals (30 × 48 inches and up) become the focal point of a main bathroom or entrance.

Where to hang an oval mirror

Above a basin, centre the oval on the tap. Above a dresser or console, choose an oval about two-thirds the width of the furniture and hang it 6 to 10 inches above the top. In a hallway, a tall oval at eye level makes a narrow space feel wider.

Quick buying tips

  • For a single basin, an oval about two-thirds the basin width looks balanced.
  • Slim ovals fit beside doors and between cabinets.
  • Gold or black frames pick up the finish of your taps.

Oval Mirrors: questions

Quick answers. Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp.

Is an oval mirror good for a small bathroom?

Yes. The curved shape takes up less visual space than a rectangle, and portrait hanging draws the eye up.

Do oval mirrors come with LED lights?

Most of ours do, backlit or with a front-lit arc. We also make framed ovals without lights.

What is the difference between oval and capsule?

An oval curves all the way round; a capsule has straight sides and rounded ends.

Can you make an oval in a custom size?

Yes, send the width and height in inches for a quote.

What is the price of an oval mirror in Pakistan?

Our oval mirrors start from Rs 6,000. Backlit LED ovals in standard bathroom sizes are around Rs 15,000 to Rs 26,500; framed LED ovals run to Rs 35,500 for the largest size.

Is an oval or round mirror better?

An oval gives more height and suits single basins and narrow walls; a round mirror is more relaxed and suits wider walls and console tables.
