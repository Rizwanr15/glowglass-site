Setting up a makeup station

Place the Hollywood mirror on a table at least 18 inches deep so you can sit close. Keep a plug point within reach and avoid a window directly behind you, which would put your face in shadow. A stool at the right height keeps your eyes level with the centre of the mirror.

For a salon or bridal makeup studio, a tall Hollywood mirror at each station lets the client see hair, makeup and outfit together.

Hollywood mirror vs LED makeup mirror

A Hollywood mirror uses round bulbs around the frame for an even, shadow-free light and a glamorous look. An LED makeup mirror, such as our arc-lit oval or front-lit round, gives similar light from a frosted band in a slimmer, wall-hung design. Choose Hollywood for a dressing room statement, LED for a clean, modern bathroom or bedroom.

Why makeup artists use Hollywood mirrors

Light from bulbs on three or four sides falls evenly across your face, so there are no shadows under the eyes or chin. That makes it easier to blend foundation and judge colour.

Sizes and styles

Dressing-table sizes start around 24 × 36 inches. Tall versions from 24 × 60 inches are used in dressing rooms and salons. Frames come in white or gold, and our triple-light model switches between warm, neutral and cool.
