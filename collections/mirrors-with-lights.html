Types of mirror lights

Backlit mirrors have an LED strip behind the glass that throws a halo onto the wall. Front-lit mirrors have a frosted band on the face of the glass that lights your face. Etched mirrors light a pattern. Hollywood mirrors use round bulbs around the frame, the classic dressing-room look.

Many of our designs combine two of these, for example front and backlit together.

Light colours and brightness

Warm white (3000K) is cosy and flattering in the evening. Neutral (4000K) is closest to daylight and best for makeup. Cool white (6000K) is the brightest and crispest. The 3-in-1 option switches between all three with a tap, and the touch sensor dims the light to the level you want.

Where to use a light mirror

Bathroom: with anti-fog so the glass stays clear after a shower. Bedroom: as a dressing mirror or a soft night light. Dressing table: front-lit or Hollywood for makeup. Hallway: a backlit mirror makes a dark corridor feel brighter and wider.

Light mirror price in Pakistan

Lit mirrors start from Rs 4,500 for small sizes. Standard bathroom sizes are about Rs 9,000 to Rs 26,500, full-length LED mirrors start at Rs 11,250, and framed LED and Hollywood designs run to about Rs 41,600.

Quick buying tips

  • Front-lit designs are best for makeup and shaving; backlit designs for mood lighting.
  • Choose 3-in-1 light if you want warm, neutral and cool in one mirror.
  • Plan a power point behind or next to the mirror before fitting.

What is the price of a light mirror in Pakistan?

Our mirrors with lights start from Rs 4,500. Standard bathroom sizes cost about Rs 9,000 to Rs 26,500, and large framed or full-length lit mirrors run to about Rs 41,600.

How long do mirror LED lights last?

Good LED strips are rated for tens of thousands of hours. The strip, driver and touch sensor are covered by our 1-year warranty.

Can I control the brightness?

Yes. Hold the touch sensor to dim or brighten. On 3-in-1 models, a tap changes the colour.

Do light mirrors use a lot of electricity?

No. LED mirrors use about as much as one or two household LED bulbs.
