What does a Bluetooth mirror do?

A Bluetooth mirror has a small speaker built in behind the glass. Pair it with your phone once and it plays music, podcasts, recitation or calls while you get ready. The speaker runs from the mirror’s own power, so there is nothing extra to charge or place on the counter.

Bluetooth LED mirrors for bathrooms and vanities

In a bathroom, pair Bluetooth with an anti-fog pad so the glass stays clear while you listen. For a vanity or dressing area, a Hollywood-style or front-lit mirror with Bluetooth makes getting ready more enjoyable. Full-length Bluetooth mirrors suit bedrooms and dressing rooms.

Shapes available with Bluetooth

Bluetooth is available on round, rectangle, square, capsule, arch, irregular and full-length designs. You can add it when you choose the options on any product listed here.

Quick buying tips

  • The speaker pairs once and reconnects automatically.
  • Combine with anti-fog for a bathroom.

How does the Bluetooth speaker work?

It sits behind the glass and pairs with your phone like any Bluetooth speaker. It runs off the mirror’s own power.

Can I add Bluetooth to any mirror?

It is available on the LED models listed on this page; ask us about others.

Is the speaker safe in a bathroom?

Yes, it sits sealed behind the glass. Keep the mirror out of direct shower spray.

Can I take calls on a Bluetooth mirror?

You can play call audio through the speaker; the microphone stays on your phone.
