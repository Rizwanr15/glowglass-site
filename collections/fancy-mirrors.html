Shapes that make a statement

Cloud, petal, pebble and pond mirrors have soft, organic outlines. Wavy and asymmetric mirrors ripple along their edges. The Eclipse mirror is cut from two offset curves. Lit from behind, each outline is drawn on the wall in light.

Patterns in light

Etched designs such as the lattice, sunburst and swirl rings, or the vine, ornament and pixel rectangles, glow when switched on, so the mirror is decor in its own right.

Decorative mirror sets

Sets of small round, rhombus or mixed mirrors come in 2, 4, 6 or 9 pieces. Arrange them in a grid above a bed or in a cluster beside a staircase.

Fancy mirror prices

Decorative sets start from Rs 2,199, organic LED shapes from Rs 4,500, etched round mirrors from Rs 6,000, and large pond-shaped mirrors with Bluetooth from Rs 32,500.

Quick buying tips

  • Give a fancy mirror a plain wall so the shape stands out.
  • Warm light brings out etched patterns; cool light suits geometric designs.
