Why choose a framed mirror

A frame protects the edge of the glass from chips, adds a design line around the mirror and makes it easier to match fittings. Aluminium is light, does not rust in bathrooms and keeps its colour.

Framed LED vs plain framed

Framed LED mirrors add backlighting or a lit band inside the frame. Plain framed mirrors need no power point and can hang anywhere, from bathrooms to bedrooms and entrances.

Prices

Plain framed rectangle mirrors start around Rs 18,500, framed LED mirrors from Rs 20,500, and framed full-length mirrors from about Rs 15,999 to Rs 39,600.

Quick buying tips

  • Match the frame to your taps and handles.
  • Black frames suit industrial and modern rooms; gold suits classic and warm interiors.
