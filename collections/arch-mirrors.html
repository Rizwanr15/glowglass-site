Gold, black and LED arch mirrors

Gold arch mirrors bring warmth to entrances and bedrooms, black arches suit modern bathrooms, and LED arches add a soft glow that outlines the curve. Our framed arched LED mirror combines a slim frame with a backlight.

Large, wide and slim arches

A slim arch (18 × 30 to 24 × 42 inches) fits narrow walls beside doors or between cabinets. Standard bathroom arches run from 24 × 36 to 36 × 60 inches. Wide half-moon mirrors, which are wider than they are tall, sit well above a console table or a long vanity.

Where arch mirrors look best

Above a console table in an entrance, an arch adds height and a sense of architecture. Above a fireplace or a dresser, choose an arch about two-thirds the width of the piece below. In a bathroom, a tall arch above a single basin works like a window.

Arch mirror sizes and prices

Slim LED arches come in 18 × 30, 18 × 36 and 24 × 42 inches, from Rs 10,350. Framed arched LED mirrors run from 24 × 36 to 36 × 60 inches, Rs 20,500 to Rs 35,500. Half-moon and semi-circle designs suit wide walls above consoles and long vanities.
