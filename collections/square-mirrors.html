Square LED mirrors for bathrooms

A square LED mirror suits compact bathrooms and twin basins. Two matching squares above a double vanity look tidier than one long mirror and give each person their own lit mirror. Rounded corners soften the look without losing the square shape.

Framed square mirrors for walls

Without lights, a framed square in gold or PVC is a simple way to add a mirror to a bedroom, study or kids’ room. Small 12 × 12 inch squares can be grouped into a grid for a feature wall.

Square mirror sizes and where they fit

A 12 × 12 or 18 × 18 inch square works as an accent mirror in a study, kids’ room or beside a dressing table, and several can be hung as a grid. 24 × 24 inches is the standard bathroom square above a single basin. 30 × 30 to 36 × 36 inches suits a main bathroom or a bedroom wall, and 42 to 48 inch squares are large enough to anchor a lounge wall or an entrance.

Hang a bathroom square with its centre at eye level, roughly 60 to 65 inches from the floor. For a grid of small squares, keep equal gaps of 2 to 3 inches and plan the layout with paper templates first.

Lit or plain: which square should you choose?

Choose a square LED mirror if the room needs light at the mirror, for shaving, makeup or a windowless bathroom. Choose a framed square if the mirror is decorative or the wall has no power point. LED squares come with a touch dimmer, three light colours and optional anti-fog; framed squares need only a hook.

Quick buying tips

  • For twin basins, hang two matching squares rather than one long mirror.
  • Rounded corners soften a square without losing its shape.

Square Mirrors: questions

What size square mirror suits a single basin?

A 24 × 24 or 30 × 30 inch square suits most single basins.

Do you have square mirrors without lights?

Yes, framed squares in PVC or gold.

Can a square LED mirror have anti-fog?

Yes, as an option on LED models.

What is the price of a square mirror?

Framed squares start from about Rs 3,200; square LED mirrors start from Rs 8,499.

Can I hang two square mirrors over a double vanity?

Yes, it is a popular layout. Leave 4 to 6 inches between them and centre each above its basin.
