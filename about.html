About GlowGlass

We make LED mirrors to the size of your wall, in Lahore.

GlowGlass started with a simple frustration: most LED mirrors sold in Pakistan come in a handful of fixed sizes, and the wall you actually have is never one of them. A 24 × 36 inch mirror looks lost over a 48 inch vanity, and a 36 inch round one will not fit between two cabinets set 32 inches apart. So we cut every mirror to order.

How we make a mirror

Every order follows the same five steps in our Lahore workshop:

  1. Cutting. We start with 5 mm silver-backed glass and cut it to your width, height and shape. Curves for round, oval, capsule and irregular mirrors are cut and then edge-polished so there is no sharp line.
  2. Sandblasting or backlighting. Front-lit designs get a frosted band where the light shows through. Backlit designs are mounted on a frame that sits a few centimetres off the wall so the light spreads behind the glass.
  3. Wiring. We fit the LED strip in the colour temperature you chose (warm 3000K, neutral 4000K or cool 6000K), a driver and a touch sensor. Bathroom models get an anti-fog pad behind the glass.
  4. Testing. Each mirror is switched on and left running before it is packed, so problems with the LEDs or sensor show up in the workshop and not on your wall.
  5. Packing and fitting. We pack with corner guards and a rigid crate for courier orders. In Lahore, our own team delivers and fits.

What we stand for

  • Any size, at a clear price. Standard sizes have prices on every product page, and custom sizes are quoted before you pay anything.
  • Light that flatters. We help you pick the right colour temperature for the room, not just the brightest strip.
  • Honest timelines. Most mirrors are ready in about 7 days. If a design will take longer, we say so up front.
  • Support after delivery. Every LED mirror has a 1-year warranty on the strip, driver and touch sensor. Read the warranty and returns policy.

Where we deliver

Delivery and fitting are free in Lahore. We also send mirrors by insured courier to Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan and other cities. The delivery and installation page covers packing, charges and what you need to prepare before we arrive.
