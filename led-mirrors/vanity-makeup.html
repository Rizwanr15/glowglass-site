Why makeup looks different in daylight

Most of us have done it: finished makeup in the bedroom, stepped outside and realised the foundation is a shade off or the blush is uneven. The cause is usually the light. A ceiling bulb lights the top of your head and leaves shadows under your eyes and jaw. A vanity mirror with lights puts the light in front of you, at face level, so what you see in the mirror is what everyone else will see.

What makes a good vanity mirror with lights

Three things matter more than the shape:

  • Even light from the front. Lights around the edge of the mirror, or bulbs on both sides, remove shadows under the eyes and chin.
  • The right colour of light. Daylight white (around 6000K) is best for foundation and colour matching. Warm white (3000K) shows how makeup will look at an evening event or under restaurant lighting. Our LED vanity mirrors let you switch between both.
  • Enough brightness, with a dimmer. You want it bright for detailed work like eyeliner and softer for everyday use.

Where to put your vanity mirror

Place the mirror where you can sit or stand facing it, with your face 30 to 60 cm from the glass. Avoid putting it directly opposite a window: strong daylight behind you will put your face in shadow. If your dressing table is in the bedroom, choose warm light for evenings and daylight white when you do your makeup.

How to choose the right vanity mirror size

Measure the dressing table or counter first. The mirror should usually be a little narrower than the table, so the table frames it. For an 36-inch dressing table, a mirror 24 to 30 inches wide looks right. For a 48-inch table, choose 36 to 42 inches. If you sit while doing makeup, the centre of the mirror should be roughly at your eye level when seated, which is usually 45 to 50 inches from the floor.

LED vs bulb vanity mirrors

LED strip mirrors give smooth, even light and a modern look; they're slim and suit small dressing tables. Bulb (Hollywood) mirrors are brighter and give that classic salon feel, but they take up more space. For everyday makeup at home, an LED mirror is usually enough. For bridal makeup, photography or professional use, choose bulbs.

Caring for a vanity mirror

Dust bulbs and the glass weekly with a soft dry cloth, and clean makeup marks with a little glass cleaner on a microfibre cloth. Keep sprays like hairspray and setting spray away from the mirror where possible; they leave a film that dulls the reflection.
