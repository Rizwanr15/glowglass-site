4c:T1716,<h2>Why an LED bathroom mirror beats a mirror and a tube light</h2>
<p>Most bathrooms in Pakistan still have the same setup: a plain mirror over the basin and a tube light or a single bulb somewhere above it. The light comes from one direction, so half your face is in shadow when you shave, tie a hijab or put on kajal. An LED bathroom mirror fixes that. The light sits in the mirror itself, all the way around the glass, so it lands on your face evenly from the front.</p>
<p>There&#39;s a practical side too. You lose one fitting, one switch and one more thing to dust. The mirror becomes the main light over the wash basin, and with the warm setting at night you don&#39;t need the harsh overhead light at all.</p>
<h2>What comes with every GlowGlass bathroom mirror</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Touch sensor:</strong> one tap turns it on, a long press dims it, a double tap changes the colour.</li>
<li><strong>Three light colours:</strong> warm white (3000K) for evenings, natural (4000K) for everyday use, and cool white (6000K) when you need to see every detail.</li>
<li><strong>Anti-fog pad:</strong> a thin heating pad behind the glass keeps the centre clear after a hot shower. In Lahore&#39;s humid summer and monsoon months, this is the feature people thank us for.</li>
<li><strong>Optional extras:</strong> a Bluetooth speaker for music or azaan alerts, and a digital clock in the corner of the glass.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Backlit, front-lit or both?</h2>
<p>This changes the look more than the shape does, so decide it first.</p>
<p>A <strong>backlit bathroom mirror</strong> has the LED strip hidden behind the glass. Light spills onto the wall and makes a glowing halo around the edge. On marble or textured tiles it looks like a hotel bathroom. It&#39;s mainly mood lighting.</p>
<p>A <strong>front-lit mirror</strong> has a frosted band etched into the glass, and the light shines straight through it towards you. Choose this if the mirror is your main light for grooming.</p>
<p>Our <a href="/products/duo-front-backlit-rectangle-mirror">Duo Front &amp; Backlit Mirror</a> does both, with each light on its own control. If you&#39;re unsure, start there.</p>
<h2>Choosing the right size for your wash basin</h2>
<p>Keep the mirror a little narrower than the basin or vanity below it. A 24-inch basin looks right with a 20 to 24-inch wide mirror. For a double vanity, use one long landscape mirror (around 48 x 30 inches) or two matching mirrors, one above each tap.</p>
<p>Hang the centre of the mirror close to eye level, about 60 to 65 inches from the floor, and leave 5 to 10 inches between the top of the tap and the bottom of the glass. If your family has very different heights, choose a taller mirror rather than a wider one.</p>
