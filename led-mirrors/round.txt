39:Tfe3,<h2>Why curved mirrors are so popular</h2>
<p>Most rooms are full of straight lines: doors, windows, tiles, cupboards. A round or arched mirror breaks that up and makes a space feel softer and more designed. It&#39;s one of the simplest ways to make a bathroom or entrance look modern, which is why round LED mirrors are our bestselling shape.</p>
<h2>Round LED mirrors</h2>
<p>A round LED mirror with a backlight creates a glowing ring on the wall, almost like an eclipse. It looks best on textured tiles, marble or a painted feature wall.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong><a href="/products/halo-round-led-mirror">Halo Round LED Mirror</a>:</strong> frameless and backlit, from 24 to 60 inches in diameter.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/products/round-framed-mirror">Round Framed Mirror</a>:</strong> a thin metal ring frame, no lighting, in black, gold, silver, rose gold or champagne.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/products/framed-backlit-led-mirror">Framed Backlit LED Mirror</a>:</strong> a brushed aluminium frame with a backlight, for when you want both.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/products/wavy-circle-led-mirror">Wavy Circle LED Mirror</a>:</strong> a circle with a soft scalloped edge, good for dressing tables.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Arch and half moon mirrors</h2>
<p>Arch mirrors have straight sides and a curved top, like a window or doorway. They look elegant in entrances and bedrooms, and the taller sizes double as full length mirrors.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong><a href="/products/half-moon-led-mirror">Half Moon LED Mirror</a>:</strong> backlit, from 30 x 48 up to 36 x 72 inches.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/products/arch-window-framed-mirror">Arch Window Framed Mirror</a>:</strong> a slim metal frame inspired by window panes.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/products/semi-circle-led-mirror">Semi Circle LED Mirror</a>:</strong> a half circle that sits flat along a console, basin or dressing table.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Organic and irregular shapes</h2>
<p>Free-form mirrors with a pebble or cloud-like outline are a big trend in modern interiors. The <a href="/products/pebble-irregular-led-mirror">Pebble Irregular LED Mirror</a> has a backlight that follows its curves, so it looks like it&#39;s floating off the wall.</p>
<h2>Choosing the right size</h2>
<p>For a round mirror above a basin, choose a diameter slightly smaller than the basin or vanity width: a 24 or 30-inch round suits most single basins. In an entrance or living room, go bigger. A 36 to 48-inch round makes a strong focal point above a console table. Arch mirrors work best tall and narrow; a 30 x 48 inch arch suits most walls.</p>

<p>The Halo Round starts at Rs 8,499 for 24 inches and goes up to Rs 32,499 for 60 inches. Wavy and semi-circle mirrors start at Rs 6,000. Half moon LED mirrors range from Rs 20,000 to Rs 32,000, and framed arch mirrors from Rs 10,400. Fitting is free in Lahore.</p>
<h2>Round mirrors in the bathroom</h2>
<p>A round mirror above a basin looks best when its diameter is a little smaller than the basin or vanity width. For a 24-inch vanity, choose a 20 to 24-inch round; for a 30-inch vanity, a 24 or 30-inch round. Centre it above the tap, with the bottom edge 5 to 10 inches above the tap. In a double vanity, two round mirrors side by side create a balanced, symmetrical look.</p>
<h2>Round mirrors in living rooms and entrances</h2>
<p>Round mirrors make excellent focal points. Above a console table in the entrance, choose a mirror about two-thirds the width of the console. Above a sofa, a large 36 to 48-inch round reflects light from windows and makes the room feel larger. Gold or black framed rounds add a decorative accent; backlit rounds add a soft evening glow.</p>
<h2>Arches and organic shapes in modern homes</h2>
<p>Arched and organic mirrors are a strong trend in Pakistani homes, especially in new houses in DHA, Bahria Town and Gulberg. Their curves balance the hard lines of tiles, cupboards and doors. Use them where you want the mirror to be noticed: an entrance, a master bathroom or a bedroom wall.</p>
