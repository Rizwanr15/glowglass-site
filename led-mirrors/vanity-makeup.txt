3c:T1200,<h2>Why makeup looks different in daylight</h2>
<p>Most of us have done it: finished makeup in the bedroom, stepped outside and realised the foundation is a shade off or the blush is uneven. The cause is usually the light. A ceiling bulb lights the top of your head and leaves shadows under your eyes and jaw. A vanity mirror with lights puts the light in front of you, at face level, so what you see in the mirror is what everyone else will see.</p>
<h2>What makes a good vanity mirror with lights</h2>
<p>Three things matter more than the shape:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Even light from the front.</strong> Lights around the edge of the mirror, or bulbs on both sides, remove shadows under the eyes and chin.</li>
<li><strong>The right colour of light.</strong> Daylight white (around 6000K) is best for foundation and colour matching. Warm white (3000K) shows how makeup will look at an evening event or under restaurant lighting. Our LED vanity mirrors let you switch between both.</li>
<li><strong>Enough brightness, with a dimmer.</strong> You want it bright for detailed work like eyeliner and softer for everyday use.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Hollywood vanity mirrors</h2>
<p>The <a href="/products/hollywood-vanity-mirror">Hollywood Vanity Mirror</a> is the classic backstage design: dimmable bulbs around a black or white frame. It&#39;s the mirror you see in bridal salons and beauty parlours, and it gives the brightest, most flattering light we make. Popular sizes are 24 x 36 and 36 x 36 inches. A Bluetooth speaker can be added.</p>

<ul>

<li><strong><a href="/products/duo-front-backlit-rectangle-mirror">Duo Front &amp; Backlit Mirror</a>:</strong> a larger rectangle with a frosted front light band, good for shared dressing rooms.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Mirrors for salons and beauty parlours</h2>
<p>We make vanity mirrors for salons, bridal studios and barbershops in sets, so every station matches. Hollywood bulb mirrors and front-lit rectangles are the most popular for salon mirror designs because they light the client&#39;s face evenly. Message us with the number of stations and the wall sizes for a bulk quote.</p>

<p>Compact LED makeup mirrors start from Rs 4,500 for an 18 x 24 inch size. Dressing-table sizes are usually between Rs 6,000 and Rs 12,500. Hollywood bulb mirrors cost between Rs 28,500 and Rs 35,500 depending on size. All prices include the LED lights, driver and wall fixings, and fitting is free in Lahore.</p>
<h2>Where to put your vanity mirror</h2>
<p>Place the mirror where you can sit or stand facing it, with your face 30 to 60 cm from the glass. Avoid putting it directly opposite a window: strong daylight behind you will put your face in shadow. If your dressing table is in the bedroom, choose warm light for evenings and daylight white when you do your makeup.</p>
<h2>How to choose the right vanity mirror size</h2>
<p>Measure the dressing table or counter first. The mirror should usually be a little narrower than the table, so the table frames it. For an 36-inch dressing table, a mirror 24 to 30 inches wide looks right. For a 48-inch table, choose 36 to 42 inches. If you sit while doing makeup, the centre of the mirror should be roughly at your eye level when seated, which is usually 45 to 50 inches from the floor.</p>
<h2>LED vs bulb vanity mirrors</h2>
<p>LED strip mirrors give smooth, even light and a modern look; they&#39;re slim and suit small dressing tables. Bulb (Hollywood) mirrors are brighter and give that classic salon feel, but they take up more space. For everyday makeup at home, an LED mirror is usually enough. For bridal makeup, photography or professional use, choose bulbs.</p>
<h2>Caring for a vanity mirror</h2>
<p>Dust bulbs and the glass weekly with a soft dry cloth, and clean makeup marks with a little glass cleaner on a microfibre cloth. Keep sprays like hairspray and setting spray away from the mirror where possible; they leave a film that dulls the reflection.</p>
