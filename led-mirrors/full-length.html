Why every home needs one full length mirror

A small mirror shows your face. A full length mirror shows the whole outfit: whether the kameez hangs right, the shoes match, and the dupatta falls the way you want. It's the last thing most people check before leaving the house, and once you have a good one you'll wonder how you managed without it.

Full length mirrors also make rooms feel bigger. A tall mirror on a bedroom wall or at the end of a corridor reflects light and space, which helps a lot in smaller apartments.

Wall-mounted, leaning or free-standing?

  • Wall-mounted: fixed flat to the wall. It's the safest choice for homes with children and the best way to save floor space.
  • Leaning: a large mirror resting on the floor and leaning against the wall at a slight angle. It looks relaxed and modern, but it should be secured with a safety bracket.
  • Free-standing (standing mirror): a mirror with its own stand at the back, so you can move it between rooms. Our Standing Framed LED Mirror comes in gold, rose gold, matt black or silver frames.

Full length mirrors with LED lights

An LED full length mirror has lights along its edges, so you can check an outfit in even light even when the room is dim. It doubles as a soft bedroom light at night. Our Full Length LED Mirror has a touch dimmer and three light colours, so you can check how an outfit looks in warm evening light or bright daylight.

Prefer a plain mirror? The Standing Framed Mirror has the same frame and stand without lighting.

Choosing the right size

The standard sizes are 5 feet (60 inches) and 6 feet (72 inches) tall, in widths of 2, 2.5 and 3 feet.

  • Height: a mirror about half your height shows your full body if it's hung at the right level. A 5-foot mirror suits most people; choose 6 feet if anyone in the family is tall or if you want room for footwear and headwear.
  • Width: 24 inches is enough for one person. Choose 30 or 36 inches if two people will use it together, or if it's the main mirror in a dressing room.
  • Narrow walls: the Slim Full Length LED Mirror starts at just 18 inches wide, which suits wardrobe doors and corridors.

Where to place a full length mirror

The most popular places are the bedroom wall beside the wardrobe, the dressing room, and the entrance or corridor wall. Place it where light falls on you, not behind you, so you're not standing in shadow. Opposite a window works well during the day.

Large mirrors are heavy and fragile, so we deliver them in crates with corner guards. In Lahore our team carries the mirror in, fixes it to the wall or sets up the stand, and secures leaning mirrors with a safety bracket. We deliver to other cities by insured courier.

How to hang a full length mirror

For a wall-mounted full length mirror, the bottom edge usually sits 6 to 12 inches above the floor, so the top lands just above head height. Leaning mirrors rest on the floor and lean back at a gentle angle; always use a safety bracket to stop them sliding or tipping. On brick and concrete walls, we use heavy-duty plugs and steel brackets. On drywall or board, we fix into the frame behind it.

Do full length mirrors make you look thinner?

A good full length mirror shouldn't change how you look. Cheap thin glass can bend slightly and distort your reflection, making you look wider or narrower. We use 5 mm silver-backed glass that stays flat, so the reflection is accurate. Leaning a mirror at a steep angle can also slightly change the reflection, so keep the lean gentle.

Full length mirrors for bridal and formal wear

For weddings and formal events, you need to see heavy dupattas, lehngas and sherwanis from head to toe. A 30 or 36-inch wide, 6-foot mirror gives enough room to turn and check the back. Warm and daylight settings help you see how embroidery and colours will look at an indoor venue or in outdoor photos.
