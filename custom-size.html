A mirror cut to your wall

Standard mirror sizes rarely match real homes. There's a geyser pipe on one side, a towel rail on the other, or a niche the builder left at an odd width. A custom size LED mirror solves that: tell us the width and height in inches, and we cut the glass to fit.

How to measure your space

  1. Measure the width available, wall to wall or between fixtures. Leave about 2 inches clear on each side.
  2. Measure the height. Over a basin, measure from 5 to 10 inches above the tap up to where you want the top of the mirror.
  3. Check where the power point or wire is. The mirror must cover it.
  4. Take a photo of the wall. It helps us suggest the right shape and size.

Shapes we make to measure

  • Rectangle and square: any width and height.
  • Round: any diameter from 18 inches upwards.
  • Arch: straight sides with a rounded top.
  • Capsule and oval: tall, soft shapes for narrow walls.
  • Organic: free-form outlines on request.

Choosing the light

Pick warm white (3000K) for a cosy feel, natural (4000K) for everyday use, or cool white (6000K) for makeup and shaving. You can also choose a 3-in-1 setting that switches between all three. Bathroom mirrors include an anti-fog pad, and a Bluetooth speaker can be added.

How custom mirror pricing works

The price depends on the area of glass, the shape and the extras. Curved shapes cost more to cut than rectangles, and very large sizes need thicker glass and extra care in delivery. As a guide, a custom rectangle costs about the same as the nearest standard size in our shop. Send us your size and we'll give you an exact price the same day.

Large and extra-large LED mirrors

We make big LED mirrors for gyms, salons, walk-in dressing rooms and hotel bathrooms, up to about 6 feet in one piece. Larger walls can be covered with matching panels. Big mirrors are delivered in a crate and installed by our team.

Mirror price per square foot in Pakistan

Plain mirror glass is often sold by the square foot, but an LED mirror's price also includes the LED strip, driver, touch sensor, anti-fog pad, cutting and finishing. That's why we quote per mirror rather than per square foot. As a rough guide, our custom LED mirrors cost about the same as the nearest standard size in our shop, and simple rectangles cost less than curved shapes of the same area.

Common custom requests

  • Between fixtures: mirrors cut to fit between a geyser pipe, towel rail or window.
  • Extra-wide vanities: one long mirror across a 6 or 7-foot counter.
  • Low ceilings: shorter, wider mirrors for bathrooms with a low ceiling or a sloping roof.
  • Matching pairs: two identical mirrors for double vanities or either side of a bed.
  • Commercial spaces: salons, gyms, clinics and hotel bathrooms needing several matching mirrors.
