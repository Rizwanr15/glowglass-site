What is an LED mirror?

An LED mirror is a mirror with LED lights built into or behind the glass. The light falls evenly on your face from the front, so there are no shadows when you shave, do your makeup or get dressed. Most of our mirrors also have a touch sensor to switch the light on, dim it and change its colour, and bathroom models include an anti-fog pad that keeps the glass clear after a hot shower.

LED mirrors vs ordinary mirrors

An ordinary mirror only reflects the light that's already in the room, so you see yourself in whatever shadows the ceiling light creates. An LED mirror adds light at face height, from the front, which is why it's so much better for shaving, skincare, makeup and getting dressed. It also replaces a separate wall light above the basin or dressing table, which means one less fitting to buy and one less switch on the wall.

LED mirrors are also efficient. A typical GlowGlass mirror uses about as much electricity as one energy-saver bulb, and it runs happily on a UPS or inverter during load-shedding.

Choosing your mirror in three steps

  1. Room and purpose: bathroom (choose anti-fog), dressing table or salon (choose front light), bedroom (choose full length), or decoration (choose framed or backlit).
  2. Shape: rectangle and square for a classic look, round, capsule and arch for a softer modern look, or organic for a statement.
  3. Size and light: measure the space and pick warm, natural or daylight-white light, or the 3-in-1 option.
