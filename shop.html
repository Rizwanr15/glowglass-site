How to choose

For a bathroom, pick a model with an anti-fog pad and a touch dimmer. Rectangle and capsule shapes suit wide vanities; round and oval suit single basins. See bathroom LED mirrors.

For a dressing area, a Hollywood-style or front-lit mirror in neutral light shows makeup colours most accurately. See vanity and makeup mirrors.

For a bedroom or entrance, a full-length mirror or a statement round mirror works well. For a decorative piece without lights, browse framed and decor mirrors.
