Frequently asked questions

Quick answers. Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp.

How much does an LED mirror cost in Pakistan?

Our LED mirrors start from Rs 4,500 for compact sizes; most bathroom sizes are Rs 13,000–26,000. The final price depends on size, shape and features like anti-fog and a dimmer. Send your size on WhatsApp for an exact quote.

Can you make a mirror in my exact size?

Yes. Every mirror is made to measure. Give us the width and height in inches and we cut the glass to fit.

How long does delivery take?

Most custom mirrors are ready in about 7 days. Delivery and fitting in Lahore are free; we ship to other cities across Pakistan.

Do the mirrors stop fogging in the bathroom?

Bathroom models include an anti-fog pad behind the glass, so the mirror stays clear after a hot shower.

What light colours are available?

Warm (3000K), neutral (4000K) and cool daylight (6000K). Many models switch between all three with the touch sensor.

Is fitting included?

Yes, delivery and fitting are free in Lahore. In other cities a local electrician or carpenter can fit the mirror using the fixings and guide we include, and we can help over a WhatsApp video call.

Does an LED mirror need a power point?

Yes. It needs a power point near the mirror, or a wire brought out behind its position for a hidden connection. An electrician can add one before fitting.

Can I add a Bluetooth speaker?

Yes, on most LED models, as an optional extra. The speaker sits behind the glass and pairs with your phone.

Can I dim the light?

Yes. Models with a touch sensor let you switch on and off and dim by holding the sensor.

Can I change the light colour after fitting?

On colour-changing models, yes: a tap on the sensor cycles through warm, neutral and cool light. Single-colour models stay in the colour you choose when ordering.

What frame colours are available?

Each framed model lists its frame finishes on the product page. Ask us if you need a finish that is not listed.

Can an LED mirror go in a bathroom?

Yes. Bathroom models have an anti-fog pad behind the glass. Keep the mirror out of the direct shower spray.

Which light colour is best for makeup?

Neutral white (around 4000K) shows skin tones most accurately. Warm light is softer for evening, and cool light is brightest.

How do I measure for a custom mirror?

Measure the width and height of the space in inches, note any sockets, taps or cabinets nearby, and send a photo of the wall. The custom size page has a step-by-step guide.

What warranty do you give?

Every LED mirror has a 1-year warranty on the LED strip, driver and touch sensor from the delivery date.

Can I return a mirror?

Custom-size mirrors cannot be returned for a change of mind because they are cut to your measurements. Standard-size items can be exchanged within 7 days if unused and in the original packing.

How do I pay?

We take an advance to start production and the balance on delivery, by bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa.
