<li><strong>Optional Bluetooth speaker.</strong></li>

<h2>Who it&#39;s for</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Makeup lovers</strong> who want salon-quality light at home.</li>

</ul>

<h2>Is the Hollywood mirror right for you?</h2>
<p>If makeup is your passion or your profession, this is the mirror. The bulbs surround your face with light from the top and both sides, so there are no shadows under the eyes, nose or chin, and colour matching is far more accurate than under a ceiling light. Bridal makeup artists, salon owners and beauty content creators all rely on this style.</p>
<p>For everyday makeup at home, a smaller LED mirror may be enough. <h2>Styling and room ideas</h2>
<p>At home, pair the Hollywood mirror with a simple dressing table, a comfortable stool and a few acrylic organisers for brushes and palettes; the bulbs do the decorating. A white frame suits light, feminine rooms, while black looks bold in modern spaces and photographs well for content creation.</p>
<p>In a salon, line several mirrors along one wall at equal heights with a counter beneath. Matching mirrors and consistent bulb brightness make every station look professional and give clients the same quality of light.</p>
