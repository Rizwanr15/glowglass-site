2d:Td5c,<h2>A frame that matches your taps</h2>
<p>The Atelier wraps the glass in a slim brushed-aluminium frame and hides an LED strip behind it. The frame gives the mirror a finished, architectural look, and the backlight adds a soft glow on the wall. Choose the frame colour to match your taps, shower and handles: matt black, gold, rose gold or silver.</p>
<h2>Features</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Brushed aluminium frame</strong> in four finishes.</li>
<li><strong>Hidden LED backlight</strong> with a soft wall glow.</li>
<li><strong>Touch sensor</strong> with dimming and colour change.</li>
<li><strong>Three light colours:</strong> warm 3000K, natural 4000K and cool 6000K.</li>
<li><strong>Anti-fog pad</strong> and <strong>optional Bluetooth speaker.</strong></li>
</ul>
<h2>Choosing a frame finish</h2>
<p>Matt black suits modern bathrooms with black fittings and grey or white tiles. Gold and rose gold suit warm, luxurious bathrooms with beige marble. Silver works with chrome taps and a clean, classic look.</p>
<h2>Sizes</h2>
<p>24 x 36 and 24 x 48 inches suit single basins. 30 x 48, 36 x 48 and 30 x 54 suit wider vanities, and 36 x 60 suits master bathrooms. Custom sizes are available.</p>
<h2>Specifications</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Shape:</strong> rectangle</li>
<li><strong>Sizes:</strong> 24 x 36, 24 x 48, 30 x 48, 36 x 48, 30 x 54 and 36 x 60 inches, or custom</li>
<li><strong>Frame:</strong> brushed aluminium in matt black, gold, rose gold or silver</li>
<li><strong>Glass:</strong> 5 mm silver-backed</li>
<li><strong>Lighting:</strong> hidden LED backlight, 3000K / 4000K / 6000K</li>
<li><strong>Controls:</strong> touch sensor with dimmer and colour change</li>
<li><strong>Bathroom features:</strong> anti-fog pad</li>
<li><strong>Options:</strong> Bluetooth speaker</li>
<li><strong>Price:</strong> Rs 20,500 to Rs 35,500</li>
</ul>
<h2>Is the Atelier right for you?</h2>
<p>The Atelier suits bathrooms where the fittings are part of the design: black taps and shower sets, brushed gold mixers, or rose gold accessories. The frame ties the mirror into that colour scheme, so the bathroom looks planned rather than pieced together.</p>
<p>If your bathroom has chrome taps and white tiles, a frameless mirror like the <a href="/products/lumen-rectangle-led-mirror">Lumen</a> may look cleaner. If you want the frame without lighting, see the <a href="/products/framed-rectangle-mirror">Framed Rectangle Mirror</a>, which costs less and needs no wiring.</p>
<h2>Ordering, delivery and care</h2>
<p>Made to order in about 7 days. Fitting is free in Lahore; other cities receive the mirror in a crate by insured courier. Wipe the frame with a soft dry cloth and avoid acid or bleach cleaners, which can dull the finish. The LEDs carry a 1-year warranty.</p>
<h2>Styling and room ideas</h2>
<p>Repeat the frame finish at least twice in the room so it looks intentional: match it to the tap and the towel rail, or the tap and the shower set. A matt black Atelier with black fittings and white or light grey tiles is the most popular combination. Gold looks warm and rich against beige marble and wood vanities. Rose gold suits softer palettes like blush, cream and light terrazzo.</p>
<p>At night, the backlight makes the frame stand out against the wall, which is especially effective on dark or textured tiles. In a double vanity, two Ateliers in the same finish look more tailored than one wide mirror.</p>
