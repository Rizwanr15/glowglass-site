About this mirror

The round mirror that goes everywhere

The Round Framed Mirror is a clean circle in a thin metal ring frame. It's one of the most versatile mirrors you can own: above a basin, a sideboard, a console table or a bed, it softens straight lines and makes a space feel finished. No lighting means no wiring, so it can hang anywhere.

Features

  • Round mirror with a thin metal ring frame.
  • Five finishes: black, gold, silver, rose gold and champagne.
  • No wiring needed.
  • 5 mm silver-backed glass.

Where it works

  • Bathroom: above a basin with taps that match the frame.
  • Entrance: above a console table.
  • Dining room: above a sideboard.
  • Bedroom: above a dressing table or bedside chest.

Sizes

24, 30 and 36 inches in diameter.

Is the Round Framed mirror right for you?

If you want one mirror that will look good in any room and never go out of style, this is it. Gold rings suit warm interiors and beige marble; black rings suit modern spaces with black taps and lights; silver suits chrome fittings and cool colours. It needs no wiring, so it's quick to install.

For a backlit round mirror, see the Halo Round. For a frame and a backlight together, choose the Framed Backlit LED Mirror.

Ordering, delivery and care

Made to order in about 7 days, with free fitting in Lahore and courier delivery across Pakistan. Wipe the frame with a dry cloth and the glass with a microfibre cloth.

Styling and room ideas

In a bathroom, match the ring to your taps: black with black, gold with brushed brass, silver with chrome. In a dining room, a 36-inch round above the sideboard reflects the dining light and makes the room feel bigger. In an entrance, pair it with a console, a lamp and a small plant.

For a modern look, hang two or three round mirrors of different sizes together on one wall. For a classic look, centre a single round mirror above the furniture below it.
