<h2>Where the Quadra works best</h2>
<p>Square mirrors suit compact bathrooms and powder rooms, where a tall rectangle would feel too big. They also look neat in pairs above twin basins: two 24 or 30-inch Quadras side by side give each person their own mirror and light.</p>
<h2>Choosing your size</h2>
<p>A 24-inch Quadra fits most small wash basins. Choose 30 or 36 inches for a standard vanity, and 42 or 48 inches if the mirror spans a wider counter. Custom sizes are available.</p>
<h2>Installation</h2>
<p>The Quadra needs a power point or wire behind the mirror and hangs on a concealed bracket. Fitting is free in Lahore.</p>

<p>The Quadra is built for people who use the mirror as a work light: shaving, skincare, contact lenses, eyebrow shaping and everyday makeup. Light comes through the frosted border straight towards you, so it&#39;s brighter on your face than a backlit mirror of the same size.</p>
