About this mirror

A soft shape for entrances and bedrooms

The Oval Framed Mirror is a simple, elegant oval in a fine metal frame. Its gentle curve softens a room, and the thin frame keeps the look light and modern. It needs no wiring, so it can go anywhere: an entrance, bedroom, dressing area or powder room.

Features

  • Oval shape in a fine metal frame.
  • Five finishes: black, gold, silver, rose gold and champagne.
  • No wiring needed.
  • 5 mm silver-backed glass.

Where it works

  • Above a console table in the entrance.
  • Above a dressing table or chest of drawers.
  • In a powder room above a pedestal basin.
  • In a hallway as a quick-check mirror.

Is the Oval Framed mirror right for you?

This is a quiet, versatile mirror that fits almost any room. Choose it for an entrance, a powder room with good light, a bedroom wall or a hallway. Its soft shape and fine frame make it easy to style with both modern and traditional furniture, and with no wiring it can go anywhere.

If you want lighting and anti-fog for a bathroom, choose the Oval LED Mirror. For other framed shapes, see the Arch Window and Round Framed mirrors.

Ordering, delivery and care

Made to order in about 7 days, with free fitting in Lahore and courier delivery across Pakistan. Wipe the frame with a dry cloth and the glass with a microfibre cloth.

Styling and room ideas

Hang the Oval Framed Mirror vertically above a console or chest of drawers, centred on the furniture, with the bottom edge 6 to 10 inches above the top surface. A gold frame suits warm interiors with wood and brass; black looks crisp in modern rooms.

In a powder room with a pedestal basin, the oval echoes the basin's curves for a soft, classic look. In a hallway, a pair of ovals in the same finish makes a long wall feel more interesting.
