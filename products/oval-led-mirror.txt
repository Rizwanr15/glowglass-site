2c:Tcbf,<h2>A classic shape with modern light</h2>
<p>Oval mirrors have been used in bathrooms and dressing rooms for generations. The Oval LED Mirror keeps that graceful, elongated shape and adds a backlight that traces the curve with a soft glow. It suits both classic and modern interiors, and looks especially good above a pedestal basin or a vanity with curved edges.</p>
<h2>Features</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Backlit oval</strong> with an even glow around the edge.</li>
<li><strong>Touch sensor</strong> for on, off, dimming and colour.</li>
<li><strong>Three light colours:</strong> warm 3000K, natural 4000K and cool 6000K.</li>
<li><strong>Anti-fog pad</strong> and <strong>optional Bluetooth speaker.</strong></li>
</ul>
<h2>Oval or capsule?</h2>
<p>Both are tall, curved shapes. The oval curves all the way around, like an egg, and feels more traditional. The <a href="/products/capsule-led-mirror">Capsule</a> has straight sides with rounded ends and feels more modern. If you like a softer, classic look, choose the oval.</p>
<h2>Sizes</h2>
<p>24 x 36 and 24 x 48 inches suit single basins. 30 x 48 and 30 x 54 suit larger vanities. 36 x 60 inches works as a statement mirror or a dressing mirror.</p>
<h2>Specifications</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Shape:</strong> elongated oval, frameless</li>
<li><strong>Sizes:</strong> 24 x 36, 24 x 48, 30 x 48, 30 x 54 and 36 x 60 inches, or custom</li>
<li><strong>Glass:</strong> 5 mm silver-backed, polished edge</li>
<li><strong>Lighting:</strong> backlit LED around the curve, 3000K / 4000K / 6000K</li>
<li><strong>Controls:</strong> touch sensor with dimmer and colour change</li>
<li><strong>Bathroom features:</strong> anti-fog pad</li>
<li><strong>Options:</strong> Bluetooth speaker</li>
<li><strong>Price:</strong> Rs 15,000 to Rs 26,500</li>
</ul>
<h2>Is the Oval right for you?</h2>
<p>The Oval suits homes with a slightly classic or traditional style: carved wooden vanities, pedestal basins, patterned floor tiles or warm beige marble. It brings the comfort of a familiar shape with the convenience of modern lighting. In modern bathrooms it also works well as a softer alternative to a rectangle.</p>
<p>If you prefer straight sides with rounded ends, choose the <a href="/products/capsule-led-mirror">Capsule</a>. If you want the oval shape without lighting, the <a href="/products/oval-framed-mirror">Oval Framed Mirror</a> has a fine metal frame and needs no wiring.</p>
<h2>Ordering, delivery and care</h2>
<p>Made to order in about 7 days. We deliver and fit for free in Lahore and ship to other cities by insured courier. Clean with a microfibre cloth and glass cleaner applied to the cloth. The LEDs are covered by a 1-year warranty.</p>
<h2>Styling and room ideas</h2>
<p>An Oval LED works beautifully above a pedestal or vessel basin, where the curves of the basin and mirror echo each other. In a classic bathroom with brass or antique gold taps, choose warm 3000K light for a gentle, timeless feel. In a modern bathroom with white tiles and black fittings, natural light keeps it fresh.</p>
<p>For a dressing room, the 36 x 60 inch Oval makes a graceful statement mirror. In a double vanity, two Ovals side by side look elegant and balanced, especially when each is centred over its own basin.</p>
