2d:Ta2b,<h2>Turn a blank wall into a feature</h2>
<p>The Decorative Wall Mirror Set is a group of small mirrors that you arrange together on a wall. It&#39;s an affordable way to fill a large wall in a lounge, stairway or entrance, bounce light around the room and add a touch of sparkle, without buying one big expensive piece.</p>
<h2>Features</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Sets of 2, 4 or 6</strong> small decorative mirrors.</li>
<li><strong>Easy to arrange</strong> in a row, a grid or a cluster.</li>
<li><strong>No wiring needed.</strong></li>
<li>Fixings included.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Arrangement ideas</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Stairway:</strong> a diagonal line of mirrors climbing with the stairs.</li>
<li><strong>Above a sofa:</strong> a row of 4 or 6 at the same height, centred on the sofa.</li>
<li><strong>Entrance:</strong> a tight cluster of 4 above a console table.</li>
<li><strong>Dining room:</strong> a grid of 6 on the long wall.</li>
</ul>
<p>Tip: lay the mirrors out on the floor first, take a photo, then mark the positions on the wall with masking tape before drilling.</p>
<h2>Specifications</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Set sizes:</strong> 2, 4 or 6 pieces</li>
<li><strong>Glass:</strong> decorative mirror glass</li>
<li><strong>Fixings:</strong> included</li>
<li><strong>Lighting:</strong> none</li>
<li><strong>Price:</strong> Rs 2,199 to Rs 5,499</li>
</ul>
<h2>Is a mirror set right for you?</h2>
<p>A mirror set is the most budget-friendly way to decorate a large wall. It&#39;s ideal for a staircase, a long corridor, a lounge wall above a sofa, or a dining room. Because you arrange the pieces yourself, you can create a straight row, a neat grid or a relaxed cluster that suits your space.</p>
<p>If you&#39;d rather have one statement piece, see the <a href="/products/round-framed-mirror">Round Framed Mirror</a> or the <a href="/products/framed-backlit-led-mirror">Framed Backlit LED Mirror</a>.</p>
<h2>Ordering, delivery and care</h2>
<p>Sets are usually ready in a few days. Fitting is free in Lahore, and we deliver across Pakistan by courier. Wipe with a soft dry cloth.</p>
<h2>Styling and room ideas</h2>
<p>Before drilling, lay the set out on the floor and try a few arrangements: a straight horizontal row above a sofa, a 2 x 3 grid in a dining room, or a loose cluster in an entrance. Keep gaps between mirrors equal, around 2 to 3 inches, for a tidy look.</p>
<p>On a staircase, follow the angle of the stairs and keep each mirror at the same height above the step below it. Mirrors near a window or lamp will reflect the most light and make the wall sparkle in the evening.</p>
