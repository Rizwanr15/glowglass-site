About this mirror

A circle with a softer edge

The Wavy Circle is a round LED mirror with a gentle wave around its edge. The scalloped outline is a popular trend in modern interiors, and it softens a room more than a plain circle. It's small enough for a dressing table and decorative enough for a hallway or bedroom wall.

Is the Wavy Circle right for you?

The Wavy Circle suits anyone who wants a round mirror with a bit more character. The soft scalloped edge is a popular trend in bedrooms and dressing areas, and it looks especially good with pastel walls, rattan furniture and plants. At its price, it's an easy way to refresh a room.

For a bathroom with anti-fog and a backlight, choose the Halo Round. For a plain round mirror with a metal frame and no wiring, see the Round Framed Mirror.

Ordering, delivery and care

Made to order in about 7 days. Fitting is free in Lahore and we deliver across Pakistan by courier. Clean with a microfibre cloth; avoid getting liquid into the edges. LEDs carry a 1-year warranty.

Styling and room ideas

The Wavy Circle loves soft, natural decor: linen, rattan, pastel paint and indoor plants. Above a white dressing table with a small vase of flowers, it creates a calm, feminine corner. In an entrance, hang it above a slim console with a tray for keys.

For a children's or teenager's room, the scalloped edge adds playfulness without looking childish, so it will still suit the room as they grow. Choose golden light for a cosy evening glow.
