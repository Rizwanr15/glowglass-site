About this mirror

A full length mirror for tight spaces

Not every home has a spare 2 or 3 feet of wall. The Slim Full Length LED Mirror starts at just 18 inches wide, so it fits beside a wardrobe, on the end of a corridor, behind a door or in a small apartment bedroom. You still get a head-to-toe reflection and LED edge lighting.

Features

  • Narrow widths from 18 inches.
  • LED edge lighting in white, golden or a warm white and golden mix.
  • Wall-mounted, so it takes no floor space.
  • Optional Bluetooth speaker.

Sizes

18 x 60, 18 x 66 and 18 x 72 inches for the narrowest walls, and 24 x 60, 24 x 66 and 24 x 72 inches for a little more width. The 66-inch height is a good middle option between 5 and 6 feet.

Where it fits

  • Between a wardrobe and the bedroom wall.
  • At the end of a corridor or in an entrance.
  • On a wall between two doors.
  • In a hostel room, studio apartment or small dressing area.

  • Mounting: wall-mounted
  • Options: Bluetooth speaker

Is the Slim Full Length right for you?

This mirror is made for real Pakistani homes, where bedrooms are often full of furniture and the only free wall is a narrow strip between the wardrobe and the door. At 18 inches wide it still shows you from head to toe, and the LED edge light means you're not getting dressed in shadow. It's also the most affordable full length LED mirror we make.

If you have more space, the 30 or 36-inch Full Length LED Mirror gives a wider view, and the Standing Framed LED Mirror can be moved around the room.

Ordering, delivery and care

Made to order in about 7 days, with free fitting in Lahore and insured courier delivery across Pakistan. Clean with a microfibre cloth. LEDs carry a 1-year warranty.

Styling and room ideas

In a hostel room, studio apartment or children's room, mount the Slim on the narrow wall beside the door, where it uses space nothing else can. In a corridor, it reflects light from other rooms and makes the passage feel wider.

Two Slims side by side, with a small gap between them, create a striking split-panel look in a dressing area. Choose warm golden light for a softer evening look, or white light if you mainly dress in the morning.
