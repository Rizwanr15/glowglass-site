About this mirror

Two kinds of light in one mirror

Most LED mirrors give you one kind of light: either a glow on the wall or light on your face. The Duo gives you both. A frosted band at the front lights your face evenly for shaving and makeup, while a backlight washes the wall in a soft glow. Each layer has its own touch control, so you can use them together or separately.

Features

  • Front-lit frosted band for clear, shadow-free task light.
  • Backlight that creates a halo on the wall.
  • Separate touch controls for each light layer, with dimming.
  • Three light colours: warm 3000K, natural 4000K and cool 6000K.
  • Anti-fog pad and optional Bluetooth speaker.

Who it's for

The Duo is ideal if the mirror is your main bathroom light, for shared bathrooms where one person does makeup and another shaves, and for dressing rooms where you want bright task light in the morning and soft mood light in the evening.

Sizes

Choose 24 x 36 or 24 x 48 inches for a single basin, 30 x 48 or 36 x 48 for wider vanities, and 30 x 54 or 36 x 60 for master bathrooms. Custom sizes are available.

Is the Duo right for you?

The Duo is our most versatile LED mirror. In the morning you can switch on the bright front light for shaving or makeup; at night, switch to the backlight alone for a soft glow that won't wake you up. It's the one we recommend most often when someone says "I want the best of both".

It costs a little more than single-light models. If you only need mood lighting, the Lumen is a better value rectangle. If you only need face lighting, the Quadra is the simplest choice.

Ordering, delivery and care

Made to order in about 7 days, with free fitting in Lahore and insured delivery across Pakistan. Clean the frosted band with a soft cloth and avoid abrasive cleaners. Both light layers are covered by our 1-year warranty.

Styling and room ideas

On marble or patterned tiles, use the backlight alone in the evening to show off the wall surface; the room instantly feels calmer. In the morning, add the front band for clear task light. This two-mode routine is why the Duo is popular in master bathrooms, where it replaces both a vanity light and a night light.

For a long double vanity, a 36 x 60 inch Duo hung landscape covers both taps with one mirror and gives each person even front light. In a dressing room, hang it portrait at eye level beside the wardrobe, and use warm light to check how evening outfits and makeup will look.

What is a backlit LED mirror?
