About this mirror

A full length mirror you can move

The Standing Framed LED Mirror has a slim aluminium frame and a fold-out rear stand, so it stands on its own anywhere in the room. Move it from the bedroom to the dressing room, or angle it towards the window for the best light. An LED backlight with a touch dimmer adds a soft glow and turns the mirror into an accent light in the evening.

Features

  • Brushed aluminium frame in gold, rose gold, matt black or silver.
  • Rear stand for free-standing use; can also be wall-mounted.
  • LED backlight with touch dimmer and colour change.
  • Three light colours: warm 3000K, natural 4000K and cool 6000K.
  • Optional Bluetooth speaker.

Sizes

24 x 60 and 24 x 72 inches suit most bedrooms. 30 x 60 and 30 x 72 give a little more width. 36 x 60 and 36 x 72 suit dressing rooms and couples.

Is the Standing Framed LED Mirror right for you?

Choose this mirror if you rent your home, move furniture often, or don't want to drill large fixings into the wall. The rear stand lets it stand anywhere, so you can put it by the window in the morning and beside the wardrobe at night. The frame and glow also make it a decorative piece in a bedroom or dressing room.

If you'd rather fix it to the wall, the frameless Full Length LED Mirror costs less. If you don't need lighting, the Standing Framed Mirror has the same frame and stand without LEDs.

Ordering, delivery and care

Made to order in about 7 days and delivered fully assembled in a crate. Fitting and setup are free in Lahore; we ship to other cities by insured courier. Wipe the frame with a dry cloth. LEDs are covered by a 1-year warranty.

Styling and room ideas

A gold-framed standing mirror looks luxurious in a bedroom with cream or beige furnishings, while matt black suits modern rooms with grey or white walls. Place it at a slight angle in a corner to make the room feel larger and to catch light from a window.

In a dressing room, put it beside a padded stool or bench where you can sit to put on shoes. For bridal preparations at home, the warm light and frame make it a natural backdrop for photos.
