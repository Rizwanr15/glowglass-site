About this mirror

The classic backstage makeup mirror

The Hollywood Vanity Mirror is the mirror you see in film dressing rooms and bridal salons: round bulbs around a solid frame, giving bright, even light straight onto your face. It's the most flattering light we make, and the best choice for detailed makeup, bridal looks and photography.

Features

  • Dimmable bulbs around the frame for bright, shadow-free light.
  • Frame in black or white.
  • Wall-mounted above a dressing table or salon counter.
  • Optional Bluetooth speaker.

Who it's for

  • Makeup lovers who want salon-quality light at home.
  • Beauty parlours and bridal studios that need matching stations.
  • Content creators who film tutorials and need even light on camera.

Sizes

24 x 36 inches (portrait) for a standard dressing table, 30 x 30 inches for a square look, and 36 x 36 inches for a large vanity or salon station.

Is the Hollywood mirror right for you?

If makeup is your passion or your profession, this is the mirror. The bulbs surround your face with light from the top and both sides, so there are no shadows under the eyes, nose or chin, and colour matching is far more accurate than under a ceiling light. Bridal makeup artists, salon owners and beauty content creators all rely on this style.

For everyday makeup at home, a smaller LED mirror may be enough. See the Asymmetric or Runway for compact dressing-table options, or the Duo for a front-lit rectangle.

Ordering, delivery and care

Made to order in about 7 days. Fitting is free in Lahore; salons outside Lahore can order matching sets delivered by insured courier. Dust the bulbs with a dry cloth and clean the glass with a microfibre cloth. The lighting is covered by a 1-year warranty.

Styling and room ideas

At home, pair the Hollywood mirror with a simple dressing table, a comfortable stool and a few acrylic organisers for brushes and palettes; the bulbs do the decorating. A white frame suits light, feminine rooms, while black looks bold in modern spaces and photographs well for content creation.

In a salon, line several mirrors along one wall at equal heights with a counter beneath. Matching mirrors and consistent bulb brightness make every station look professional and give clients the same quality of light.
