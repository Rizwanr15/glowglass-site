A tall Hollywood-style mirror with bulbs on three sides that switch between warm, neutral and cool light. It is sized for a dressing room or salon station where you need to see your whole outfit as well as your face.

What is the price of the Triple-Light Hollywood Mirror?

It starts at Rs 27,500 for 24 × 60 in and goes up to Rs 36,000 for 30 × 72 in. Prices for other sizes are quoted on request.

What sizes does the Triple-Light Hollywood Mirror come in?

Standard sizes are 24 × 60 in, 30 × 60 in, 36 × 60 in, 24 × 72 in, 30 × 72 in. We can also cut any size in between.

Can the Triple-Light Hollywood Mirror be made in a custom size?

Where is the Triple-Light Hollywood Mirror made?

In our workshop in Lahore, from 5 mm silver-backed glass, cut and finished to order.

How long does the Triple-Light Hollywood Mirror take to deliver?

Most orders are ready in about 7 days. Courier delivery to other cities usually takes 1 to 5 working days after that.

Is fitting included with the Triple-Light Hollywood Mirror?

Which rooms suit the Triple-Light Hollywood Mirror?
