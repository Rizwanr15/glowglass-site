About this mirror

Turn a blank wall into a feature

The Decorative Wall Mirror Set is a group of small mirrors that you arrange together on a wall. It's an affordable way to fill a large wall in a lounge, stairway or entrance, bounce light around the room and add a touch of sparkle, without buying one big expensive piece.

Features

  • Sets of 2, 4 or 6 small decorative mirrors.
  • Easy to arrange in a row, a grid or a cluster.
  • No wiring needed.
  • Fixings included.

Arrangement ideas

  • Stairway: a diagonal line of mirrors climbing with the stairs.
  • Above a sofa: a row of 4 or 6 at the same height, centred on the sofa.
  • Entrance: a tight cluster of 4 above a console table.
  • Dining room: a grid of 6 on the long wall.

Tip: lay the mirrors out on the floor first, take a photo, then mark the positions on the wall with masking tape before drilling.

Specifications

  • Set sizes: 2, 4 or 6 pieces
  • Glass: decorative mirror glass
  • Fixings: included
  • Lighting: none
  • Price: Rs 2,199 to Rs 5,499

Is a mirror set right for you?

A mirror set is the most budget-friendly way to decorate a large wall. It's ideal for a staircase, a long corridor, a lounge wall above a sofa, or a dining room. Because you arrange the pieces yourself, you can create a straight row, a neat grid or a relaxed cluster that suits your space.

If you'd rather have one statement piece, see the Round Framed Mirror or the Framed Backlit LED Mirror.

Ordering, delivery and care

Sets are usually ready in a few days. Fitting is free in Lahore, and we deliver across Pakistan by courier. Wipe with a soft dry cloth.

Styling and room ideas

Before drilling, lay the set out on the floor and try a few arrangements: a straight horizontal row above a sofa, a 2 x 3 grid in a dining room, or a loose cluster in an entrance. Keep gaps between mirrors equal, around 2 to 3 inches, for a tidy look.

On a staircase, follow the angle of the stairs and keep each mirror at the same height above the step below it. Mirrors near a window or lamp will reflect the most light and make the wall sparkle in the evening.
