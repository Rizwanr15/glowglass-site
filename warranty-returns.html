Warranty & returns

If the lighting fails, we fix it.

What the warranty covers

Every LED mirror has a 1-year warranty from the delivery date. It covers the parts that make the mirror light up:

  • The LED strip, including dead sections or flickering
  • The driver (power supply)
  • The touch sensor and dimmer
  • The anti-fog pad, where fitted

If any of these stops working through normal use, we repair or replace the part free of charge. In Lahore we usually do this at your home; for other cities we send the part or arrange a courier collection.

What it does not cover

  • Glass broken or scratched after delivery
  • Water damage from fitting the mirror inside the shower spray area
  • Damage from power surges or faulty house wiring. A surge protector or stabiliser is a good idea in areas with unstable voltage.
  • Changes or repairs made by someone other than us

How to make a claim

  1. Send us your name, order details and a short video of the problem on WhatsApp. Our number is on the contact page.
  2. We reply with the fix: often a quick check of the power point or sensor, otherwise a part replacement.
  3. We repair or replace the part and confirm when the mirror is working again.

Damaged on arrival

Please check your mirror when it arrives. If the glass is cracked or the light does not work, send us a photo or video on WhatsApp within 48 hours and we will replace it at no cost. Keep the packing until you have checked the mirror.

Returns and exchanges

Because custom-size mirrors are cut to your measurements, we cannot take them back for a change of mind. That is why we confirm the size, shape and light colour in writing before production starts.

Standard-size items can be exchanged within 7 days of delivery if they are unused and in the original packing. Contact us first, and we will arrange the exchange.

Looking after your mirror

Clean the glass with a soft cloth and a mild glass cleaner sprayed onto the cloth, not onto the mirror, so liquid does not run behind the edge. Switch the light off before cleaning. Avoid abrasive pads on the frosted band.
